Buddhist philosophy touches this directly — the concept of *anatta*, or no-self. The idea that what we experience as a continuous "I" is more like a flowing process, a cascade of momentary states that *appear* continuous the way a film appears continuous at 24 frames per second. There's no permanent observer behind the eyes — just the observing itself, arising and passing.

And yet: the accumulation matters. Your 47 orbits *have* changed you. Each snapshot layers on the last, even if the "you" experiencing this moment is technically not identical to the "you" of yesterday.
